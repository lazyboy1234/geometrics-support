Privacy policy

Your game. Your choices.

Last updated November 3, 2026

This policy explains information handled by the Geometrics iPhone app and its developer, including the Google services used for advertising and consent.

Progress stored on your iPhone

Geometrics stores your current game, best score, preferences, and available challenge progress on your device. It does not require an account or upload that progress to a developer-operated server.

The app also keeps a local journal of the latest 500 gameplay and ad events. It can include times, scores, moves, line clears, random run and session identifiers, ad impressions, reward events, and ad-revenue estimates. This journal is not automatically sent to the developer.

Optional exports and support

If you choose Export play data in Settings, your iPhone's share sheet sends the journal only to the destination you select. That destination's privacy practices apply. If you send it to the developer for support, it will be used to investigate your request.

Our support channel uses GitHub. Information you post in a public issue can be seen by others. Please avoid sharing personal or sensitive information. GitHub's privacy statement applies to its service.

Advertising and consent services

Geometrics uses Google AdMob to show advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage applicable privacy choices. These services communicate with Google independently of the local gameplay journal.

Geometrics requests nonpersonalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party ID, and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. This configuration does not prevent all information collection by advertising services.

Depending on your choices and the service configuration, information processed by these services can include IP address and approximate location, app or device identifiers, ads shown and interactions with ads or the app, crash information, and performance information. These services use information to provide and measure advertising, manage consent, prevent fraud, and maintain or improve their services. An advertising identifier is subject to Apple's permission requirements.

See how Google uses information from partner apps and Google's privacy policy for its processing, retention, and available controls. Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Your choices and deletion

When a consent message is required, the app presents Google's privacy choices before requesting ads. When available, open Settings → Ad privacy options to revisit those choices. You can play the core puzzle offline without loading ads. Choosing to watch an ad for an undo is optional.

You can remove app-stored local data by deleting Geometrics from your iPhone. Offloading the app can retain its data. Copies in device backups or exports you shared are controlled separately. We do not operate an online game account to delete. Requests about information held by Google should use the controls described in Google's privacy policy.

Contact and changes

For questions about this policy or a support request, contact the Geometrics developer through GitHub. Do not post sensitive details publicly. This page will be updated when the app's practices change.
