Help & support

A hand with your next move.

How do I play?

Drag a piece from the tray onto the diamond board. Fill a complete line to clear it. Use all three tray pieces to get a new set. A run ends when none of the remaining pieces fit.

Start or resume a game, then open Settings → How to play for the in-game guide. You can also tap to select a piece, then tap the board to place it.

Where is my progress?

Your game, best score, and settings save on your iPhone. There is no Geometrics account or online progress sync. Deleting the app can remove your local progress.

Sound, motion, and advertising

Settings has sound, haptics, and motion controls. When available, Ad privacy options lets you revisit advertising choices. From a game, use Report an ad in Settings to report an advertisement to Google.

Rewarded undo is optional and available once per run. If an ad is unavailable, you can keep playing or start another run.

Report a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. GitHub issues are public: leave out passwords, payment details, and other private information. Sharing the optional play-data export is your choice.
